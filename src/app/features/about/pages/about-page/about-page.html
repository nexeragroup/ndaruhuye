02 · My story

Engineering became a way of solving bigger problems.

I started with software development, but over time my work expanded beyond writing applications.

Building real systems exposed me to databases, infrastructure, networking, APIs, deployment, security, user experience, and the operational realities surrounding technology.

That changed how I approach engineering. Instead of seeing a product as a collection of screens and endpoints, I look at the complete system: the people, information, processes, integrations, and decisions that technology needs to support.

Today, that perspective extends into data and AI. I'm interested in using intelligent technology to make information easier to understand, automate repetitive work, and help people make better decisions.
